Why DictoKey for Korean?

Korean is spoken by 81 million people worldwide. Korean uses Hangul, a scientifically designed alphabet, but speech-to-text must handle honorific levels, particle usage, and the significant gap between written and spoken Korean. With DictoKey you simply speak, and the text is inserted in Hangul directly into the app you are using.

Cloud AI Speech Recognition

DictoKey sends your voice to AI speech recognition in the cloud, so an internet connection is required. In Clean mode, punctuation and capitalization are restored automatically, so a dictated Korean message is ready to send without retouching. No recordings are stored.
