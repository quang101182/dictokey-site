Typeless Android Alternative: What to Use Instead in 2026

Published November 4, 2026 · DictoKey Team · 6 min read

Short answer. The closest alternatives to Typeless on Android are Wispr Flow (also AI dictation, with a similar free-then-paid model) and Gboard voice typing (free and built in). DictoKey is a different shape: a voice keyboard plus a floating bubble that sits above the keyboard you already use, with automatic punctuation, rewriting, translation and 51 languages, but it needs an internet connection.

→ Get DictoKey on Google Play (free plan: 30 dictations per day; no account needed).

People look for a Typeless alternative for ordinary reasons: the free plan runs out, the price does not fit, they want to keep their usual keyboard, or they need a language or feature set that fits them better. This page compares the options as we found them on official pages on November 4, 2026. We make DictoKey, so we list the strengths of the others too.

What Typeless is, from its own pages

Typeless says it works across Mac, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android, and its pricing page lists an Android download. It advertises removal of filler words, handling of mid-sentence corrections, formatting of spoken lists, a personal dictionary, tone adapted to each app, translation while you speak, and 100+ languages with automatic detection. Its Free plan has limited words each week and no number is published; Pro is $12 per month billed yearly or $30 billed monthly. These are real strengths, especially if you also dictate on a computer and want the same tool everywhere.

Why people switch

  • Keeping your keyboard. If you like Gboard, Samsung Keyboard or SwiftKey for typing, a floating bubble lets you dictate without swapping keyboards. DictoKey has two independent ways to dictate, a standard Android voice keyboard and the bubble, so if one is unavailable the other still works.
  • Price and limits. Typeless and Wispr Flow both cap free use by words per week. DictoKey caps free use by dictations per day (30 at the time of writing), and Premium includes 1,000 dictations a day with Interpreter Mode.
  • Reworking text you already wrote. Drag the DictoKey bubble onto Translate, Correct or Emojis to rework what is in the field, and step back with Previous / Next.
  • Mistakes while speaking. Clean mode restores punctuation and capital letters automatically, and in the keyboard a long press on the dictation button lets you say the correction.

What DictoKey does not do

It needs an internet connection, so it will not help on a plane without Wi-Fi. It supports 51 languages, fewer than the 100+ that Typeless and Wispr Flow advertise. It is available on Android 10 and later and on Windows 10/11 with a separate subscription, but not on iOS or Mac, so if you dictate on an iPhone or a Mac, Typeless covers more of your devices. It also blocks banking apps, password managers and crypto wallets by design, so it works in most apps rather than all of them.

Who should pick what

  • Pick Typeless if you want one AI dictation tool across phone and computer, including iOS, Mac and Linux, and 100+ languages.
  • Pick Wispr Flow if you prefer its rewriting-first approach and its $12 per month annual price suits you.
  • Pick Gboard voice typing if free and already installed is enough.
  • Pick DictoKey if you want to keep your current keyboard, dictate from a bubble you can resize and anchor, translate or rework text by dragging, and start without an account. It is also a good fit for people who want to cut down on typing because of hand pain or limited dexterity, as described on our accessibility page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a good Typeless alternative for Android?

Yes. Wispr Flow is the closest like-for-like AI dictation app, Gboard voice typing is the free built-in option, and DictoKey is a voice keyboard plus a floating bubble that works above the keyboard you already use. The right one depends on whether you want to replace your keyboard, keep it, or pay nothing.

Does Typeless replace my Android keyboard?

Typeless markets itself as an AI voice keyboard, and a public Product Hunt comment from a user says it took over their phone keyboard until they changed the default keyboard in Settings. We could not confirm the exact behavior on an official page, so check it on your own phone. DictoKey offers both a standard keyboard and a separate floating bubble, so you can keep Gboard, Samsung Keyboard or SwiftKey for typing.

How much does Typeless cost?

Typeless's pricing page shows a Free plan with limited words each week (no number given) and a Pro plan at $12 per month billed yearly or $30 billed monthly. DictoKey has a free plan with 30 dictations per day, and Premium costs about $7.50 per month or about $55 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Does DictoKey work offline?

No. DictoKey transcribes speech in the cloud, so it needs an internet connection. We did not find an official statement on whether Typeless dictation runs fully offline.

How many languages does DictoKey support?

DictoKey supports 51 languages, with automatic language detection as an option. Typeless and Wispr Flow both state 100+ languages on their own pages.

Is DictoKey safe for sensitive apps?

DictoKey is GDPR compliant, stores no recordings and shows no ads. It blocks banking apps, password managers and crypto wallets automatically, and it works in most other apps. Its Accessibility permission shows the bubble, inserts your dictated text and reads the active field on your phone (for Previous / Next and spacing); that text is sent only when you drop the bubble on Translate, Correct or Emojis, and it is not stored.
