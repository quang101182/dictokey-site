How to Enable Voice Typing on Android: Gboard and Samsung

Published November 20, 2026 · DictoKey Team · 6 min read

Short answer. Open any text field and tap the microphone on your keyboard. On Gboard, the microphone is at the top of the keyboard. On a Samsung Galaxy phone you first switch voice input on under Settings, General management, Keyboard list and default. If the microphone does nothing, check the microphone permission under Settings, Apps. If you would rather dictate from a floating button above your keyboard, DictoKey offers one.

Voice typing is built into most Android phones, but it is not always switched on, and the steps differ between keyboards. This page walks through the two most common cases, Gboard and the Samsung Keyboard, then the microphone permission that both depend on. Everything below comes from the vendors’ own help pages, checked today, and menu names can differ on your phone.

Option 1: turn on voice typing in Gboard

Google’s Gboard help page gives the basic steps. Gboard needs to be installed, and Google notes that talk-to-text does not work with all languages.

  1. Open an app where you can type, such as Gmail or Keep.
  2. Tap a text field so the keyboard appears.
  3. Tap the microphone at the top of the keyboard.
  4. When you see “Speak now”, say what you want written.

A few things Google documents once you are dictating:

  • Punctuation: say “period”, “comma”, “question mark”, “new line” or “new paragraph”. These commands may not exist in every language.
  • Replace a word: touch and hold the word, tap the microphone and say the new word.
  • Pause: tap the microphone again.
  • Switch language: enable several languages in Gboard first, then tap the language abbreviation on the toolbar.
  • Voice commands: open the menu or the language abbreviation and tap “Show voice commands”.

Some Pixel models also offer advanced voice typing as a separate option. Google’s page does not say whether Gboard voice typing works offline, so we do not claim it either way.

Option 2: turn on voice input on a Samsung Galaxy

Samsung’s support page describes the steps. On a Galaxy phone the microphone may be missing until you switch voice input on:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap General management, then Keyboard list and default.
  3. Turn on the switch for Google Voice Typing. You can also turn on the Keyboard button on navigation bar switch.

To use Samsung’s own engine instead, tap Samsung Keyboard, scroll to Voice input and select the one you want. Under Offline voice input you can pick a language or install an offline language pack. Then open an app with a text field, tap the microphone icon on the keyboard toolbar and speak. The first time, you may need to accept terms or grant permissions. Samsung also states that Samsung voice input works only with the Samsung Keyboard.

If dictation starts but misbehaves, our guides to Samsung voice typing not working and Gboard voice typing not working cover troubleshooting.

The microphone permission

Voice typing cannot work if the app that records your voice is not allowed to use the microphone. Google’s Android help gives this path:

  1. Open the Settings app and tap Apps.
  2. Tap the app you want (use the full app list if you cannot find it).
  3. Tap Permissions, then Microphone.
  4. Choose Allow, or Allow only while using the app to limit access to when the app is open.

You can also go by permission type: Settings, Security & Privacy, Privacy, Permission manager, Microphone. Google adds that turning off Microphone access under Privacy controls blocks the microphone for the whole device and stops voice features from working.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I turn on voice typing on Android?

Tap any text field, then tap the microphone on your keyboard. On Gboard the microphone sits at the top of the keyboard and a “Speak now” prompt appears. On a Samsung Galaxy phone, go to Settings, General management, Keyboard list and default, and turn on Google Voice Typing or use Samsung voice input. Menu names can vary by phone and software version.

Why is there no microphone on my keyboard?

On a Galaxy phone, voice input has to be switched on in Settings under General management and Keyboard list and default. On other phones, check that you are using a keyboard with voice typing, such as Gboard, and that the microphone permission is allowed. Gboard’s help page notes that talk-to-text does not work with all languages.

How do I allow the microphone for voice typing?

Open Settings, tap Apps, choose the app, tap Permissions, then Microphone and choose Allow, or Allow only while using the app. Google’s help page also says that turning off Microphone access for the whole device, under Security and privacy, Privacy, Privacy controls, stops voice features from working.

Does voice typing on Android add punctuation?

Gboard supports spoken commands such as “period”, “comma”, “question mark” and “new line”, which may not be available in every language. DictoKey’s Clean mode restores punctuation and capital letters automatically.
