A voice note is quick to record but awkward to receive: the other person has to listen, often in a place where they cannot. Dictating gives you the speed of talking and sends plain text they can read at a glance. This page compares three things people mean by “voice to text for WhatsApp” using pages checked on November 10, 2026. We make DictoKey, so we also say where the others fit better.

Two different problems: dictating and transcribing

Dictating is you speaking so that a typed message appears in the chat box. Transcribing is turning a voice note someone sent you into text. WhatsApp announced Voice message transcripts for the second case: you go to Settings > Chats > Voice message transcripts, choose a language, then press and hold a voice message and tap transcribe. WhatsApp says transcripts are generated on your device so that no one else, not even WhatsApp, can hear or read your personal messages, and that only a few languages were available at launch, with more planned. That feature does not help you write a message, so for dictation you need your keyboard or an app.

Option 1: the Gboard microphone

According to Google’s help page, you open an app where you can type, tap the text field, tap the microphone at the top of the keyboard and, when you see “Speak now”, say what you want written. That works in the WhatsApp chat box like anywhere else. You can say period, comma, exclamation point, question mark, new line or new paragraph to insert punctuation, though these phrases may not be available in every language. To replace a word, touch and hold it, tap the microphone and say the new one. Google also notes that talk-to-text does not work with all languages. The page does not say whether it needs an internet connection.

If you already use Gboard and short messages are your main use, this is the simplest start. There is nothing to install.

Option 2: a dictation app with a floating bubble

In WhatsApp, that means you can keep your usual keyboard and tap the bubble to speak. The bubble is a two-part pill: language on one side, microphone on the other. Tap the microphone to dictate; a long press dictates and then translates (this is adjustable).

A practical routine for chatting by voice

  1. Open the chat and tap the message box so the keyboard or bubble is ready.
  2. Speak a full sentence, then pause. Short sentences are easier to check.
  3. Read the text before sending. Names and unusual words are the most common slips with any dictation tool.
  4. Use voice correction or the Correct action rather than retyping a whole line.

For long replies, dictating two or three paragraphs and reading them once is usually faster than a voice note the other person must replay. It also leaves a message you can search later.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I turn voice to text on in WhatsApp itself?

WhatsApp does not have its own dictation button for writing messages in the text box; you use the microphone on your keyboard (for example Gboard) or a dictation app. WhatsApp does have a separate feature, Voice message transcripts, which turns voice notes you receive into text. You enable it under Settings > Chats > Voice message transcripts.

What is the difference between dictating and transcribing a voice note?

Dictating means you speak and your words appear as a typed message the other person reads. Transcribing means you turn a voice note someone sent you into text. They solve opposite problems, and WhatsApp's transcripts feature only covers the second one.

Is voice typing free for WhatsApp messages?

Gboard has a built-in microphone, and we did not find a price on its help page. DictoKey has a free plan of 100 dictations a day in your first week, then 30 a day, with no account needed.

Does DictoKey work without internet?

No. DictoKey transcribes in the cloud and needs an internet connection. WhatsApp says its own transcripts are generated on your device.

Can I send a message in another language?

DictoKey supports 51 languages with auto-detect, plus real-time translation, so you can speak in one language and send text in another. Gboard's help page says voice typing does not work with all languages.

Will it work if WhatsApp is open and the keyboard closes mid-dictation?

DictoKey keeps a dictation that could not be inserted for a few minutes, in memory only, and offers to insert it in the next field you tap. It never inserts automatically.
