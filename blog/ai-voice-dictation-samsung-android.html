"AI dictation" on Android in 2026 depends heavily on which phone you own. The most talked-about new feature is tied to one phone line, the phone-maker features on Samsung solve a different problem, and the tools that work everywhere are not all built for writing. This guide sorts out what exists, on which phones, and links to the official page behind every fact.

It is written with one reader in mind in particular: someone for whom typing on a touchscreen is slow, painful or unreliable, and who needs voice to write messages, emails and notes in any app.

Gboard Rambler: Gemini dictation, on Pixel 11

Rambler is Gboard's Gemini-powered dictation. Google's support page lists it as reserved for Pixel 11 phones, available in 11 languages including French, free, and needing an internet connection for its full features (Google Pixel Help, consulted September 11, 2026).

So if you own a Samsung Galaxy, a Xiaomi, an Oppo or a Honor phone, installing Gboard does not bring Rambler with it. Availability can change, so check Google's support page for the current list.

Standard Gboard voice typing: everywhere, with Pixel extras

Gboard's regular voice typing is available on all Android phones. Google notes that its advanced voice typing features are reserved for Pixel 6 and later (Gboard Help). On a Samsung phone, you can install Gboard from Google Play and use the microphone key for basic voice typing.

Samsung Keyboard and Galaxy AI: rewriting and translation

On Galaxy phones running One UI 8 or later, Galaxy AI adds two writing tools to the Samsung Keyboard: Writing Assist, which rewrites or corrects text you have already typed, and conversation translation (Samsung Support). Both are useful. Neither is a long, continuous dictation mode: you still need to get the words into the field first.

That distinction matters if typing is the hard part for you. A tool that polishes typed text does not remove the typing.

Google Voice Access: control the phone by voice

Voice Access lets you control your phone with voice commands and includes basic dictation. Google lists it in 7 languages (Android Accessibility Help).

If you need to control the whole phone by voice, Voice Access is the tool designed for that. It solves a different problem from a dictation keyboard, and the two can be used side by side: one to move around the phone, the other to write.

How to turn on the DictoKey keyboard on a Samsung phone

  1. Install DictoKey from Google Play and allow the microphone permission when asked.
  2. Enable the keyboard. Open Settings → General management → Keyboard list and default, then switch DictoKey on. Menu names can vary slightly between One UI versions. Android shows a standard warning that a keyboard can "collect text"; it appears for every third-party keyboard.
  3. Select it in a text field. Open any app with a text field, then tap the keyboard icon in the navigation bar. On Samsung phones this switcher sometimes only appears after a long-press on the spacebar of the current keyboard.
  4. Optional: turn on the floating bubble. Enable it in DictoKey's settings and follow the on-screen steps. A disclosure screen explains what the Accessibility permission allows before anything is enabled.

Good to know: on devices where Android's Advanced Protection Mode is on, Android blocks floating bubbles system-wide. DictoKey tells you so directly, and the voice keyboard remains fully available.

On a Pixel or another brand, the keyboard setting usually sits under Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard. Our voice keyboard setup guide covers it step by step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get Gboard's Rambler dictation on a Samsung Galaxy phone?

Not according to Google's own support page. As of September 11, 2026, Google lists Rambler, the Gemini-powered dictation in Gboard, as available on Pixel 11 phones only, in 11 languages including French. Samsung and other Android brands can still use standard Gboard voice typing.

Does Galaxy AI let me dictate long texts on the Samsung Keyboard?

Galaxy AI on the Samsung Keyboard (One UI 8 and later) is built around Writing Assist, which rewrites or corrects text that is already typed, and conversation translation. Samsung does not describe it as a long, continuous dictation mode. For dictation you still need a voice keyboard or a dictation app.

Is Google Voice Access a dictation app?

Voice Access is mainly a way to control your phone with voice commands. It also includes basic dictation and is available in 7 languages. It is a good fit if you need to operate the whole phone by voice, and it can be used alongside a voice keyboard.

Does DictoKey work on Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo and Honor phones?

Yes. DictoKey works on Android phones of every brand, including Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Honor and Pixel, running Android 10 or later. It offers two ways to write by voice: a keyboard you can select in any text field, and a floating bubble that works in every app.

Does DictoKey work offline?

No. DictoKey transcribes speech in the cloud, so dictation requires an internet connection. Speech is transcribed on a server and then discarded. Gboard Rambler and Wispr Flow also need an internet connection for their full dictation features.
