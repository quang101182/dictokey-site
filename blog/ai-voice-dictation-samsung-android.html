“AI dictation” on Android depends on which phone you hold. One headline feature is tied to one phone line, the phone-maker tools on Samsung solve a different problem, and the tools that run everywhere are not all built for long dictation. This page sorts out what exists, with the official help page behind each fact.

If your Samsung voice typing is the problem, see Samsung voice typing not working and our Samsung voice typing alternative guide.

What exists on Samsung and other Android phones

  • Gboard Rambler. Google calls it a text input feature powered by Gemini in Gboard that turns spoken thoughts into clean, structured writing rather than a word-for-word transcript. The requirement listed is Pixel 11 series devices. Full features need an internet connection, offline use has limited features, and usage limits apply.
  • Standard Gboard voice typing. Google's help page says it is for Android phones and tablets using Gboard, that talk-to-text does not work with all languages, and that spoken punctuation might not be available in all languages.
  • Samsung voice input and Google Voice Typing. Samsung says there are two built-in voice-to-text options. Samsung voice input is only available with the Samsung Keyboard, and offline language packs can be installed for it.
  • Galaxy AI Writing assist. Samsung lists Chat translation, Composer, Writing style and Spelling and grammar, on select phones and tablets running One UI 6.1 or higher. The page describes typed-text help and does not mention dictation.
  • Google Voice Access. Google describes it as a way to control your device with spoken commands, including editing text hands-free, in seven languages.

Why people look beyond what comes with the phone

  • The headline feature is phone-specific. If you do not own the listed Pixel, Rambler is not an option on your Galaxy.
  • The built-in microphone lives in one keyboard. Switch keyboards and it goes with the old one.
  • Small targets. Reaching a toolbar microphone is the hard part for many people with limited hand mobility, tremors or arthritis.
  • Typing help is not speaking help. Writing assist improves text you already typed; it does not replace typing it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get Gboard's Rambler dictation on a Samsung Galaxy phone?

Google's Rambler help page lists Pixel 11 series devices as the requirement, so on a Samsung Galaxy it is not listed. Standard Gboard voice typing works on Android phones, and you can pick it as the dictation type in Gboard settings. Press reports of wider availability are unconfirmed by Google's help page.

Does Samsung have its own voice typing?

Yes. Samsung's support page says there are two built-in voice-to-text options: Samsung voice input, which is only available with the Samsung Keyboard, and Google Voice Typing. You can switch between them in the keyboard settings, and offline language packs can be installed for Samsung voice input.

Does Galaxy AI Writing assist dictate for me?

Samsung's Writing assist page describes Chat translation, Composer, Writing style and Spelling and grammar for text you have typed, on select devices running One UI 6.1 or higher. It does not describe dictation, so for speaking a message you still need a voice input tool.

Is Google Voice Access a dictation app?

Google describes Voice Access as an app that lets you control your device with spoken commands, including editing text hands-free. It supports English, Spanish, German, Italian, French, Portuguese and Japanese. It is built for operating the phone, so many people pair it with a voice keyboard.

Does DictoKey work on Samsung phones?

Yes. DictoKey runs on Android 10 and later, on Samsung and other brands. It has a voice keyboard and a floating bubble that works in most apps, so you can keep the Samsung Keyboard for typing. Banking apps, password managers and crypto wallets are blocked automatically.
