Wispr Flow: Strong English Dictation, Limited Elsewhere

Wispr Flow earned its reputation on Mac and iOS by combining accurate transcription with strong AI cleanup — removing filler words, fixing grammar, and formatting based on context. The Android version inherits those strengths. For English long-form writing — drafting emails, blog posts, voice notes that need to read cleanly — Wispr Flow is genuinely excellent.

The limits show up in three areas. First, multilingual workflow: Wispr Flow lists 100+ languages but its translation is post-dictation rather than inline. You speak in your source language, then trigger translation as a separate step. For users who chat with international contacts daily, inline translation is structurally faster.

Second, the pricing pipeline: Wispr Flow is free during Early Access but the standard price is $12/month. That puts it in SaaS territory rather than keyboard-upgrade territory. Users who want a free tier that survives need an alternative built around that promise.

Third, data residency: Wispr Flow runs on US infrastructure with audio tied to a Wispr account. For European users or anyone working in regulated sectors, EU GDPR hosting is a structural improvement.

GroqBoard: The One-Time-License Alternative

GroqBoard offers a $4.99 one-time lifetime license with no subscription — a direct response to the SaaS model of Wispr Flow. It supports 8 languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, plus auto-detect) and includes Wear OS support, which neither DictoKey nor Wispr Flow currently offer. Users bring their own free Groq API key, which keeps the pricing model sustainable.

For English-first power users who write mostly in one or two languages and want a one-time payment, GroqBoard is hard to beat on cost-of-ownership. The 8-language limit becomes the deciding factor for multilingual users.

Gboard Voice Typing: The Unbeatable Free Default

Gboard ships with most Android phones and offers free voice typing in 100+ languages. For clean English in a quiet room, Gboard's voice typing is good enough that most users never reach for an alternative. It is also unbeatable as a fallback. Even DictoKey users keep Gboard installed for swipe typing, emoji search, and GIF insertion — and switch to DictoKey for voice dictation and translation.

Gboard's gaps: voice accuracy degrades on accented or noisy speech, there is no inline translation, no AI rewriting, no floating bubble overlay. For users who hit those limits, an AI voice keyboard is the right upgrade.

Typeless: AI-Cleaned Dictation at $144/Year

Typeless is a newer entrant that focuses on AI-cleaned English dictation. The free tier allows up to 1,000 dictated words per week, with a $144/year Pro plan for unlimited use. When You Should Stay with Wispr Flow

Transparency matters. Wispr Flow is the right choice in three scenarios:

  • $12/month fits your budget. If the post-Early-Access price is acceptable and you do not need multilingual workflow or EU hosting, Wispr Flow is a polished product.
