Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short — No account, no ads. Your voice is sent to a speech-to-text service to be turned into text, then discarded: we never store your audio. We use Google Firebase to measure how the app is used and to detect crashes. You can delete your data from the app at any time.

1. What we do not collect

DictoKey does not require an account. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not show ads.

2. Voice dictation

When you dictate, the recording is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, which forwards it to a speech-to-text service:

  • Microsoft Azure (MAI-Transcribe), on servers located in the European Union (North Europe region), for most languages;
  • Groq (Whisper model, United States) for Croatian, Serbian and Lao, and for audio files you share to the app.

The audio is kept in memory only for the time of the request and is never written to storage, on your phone or on our server. If you added personal words ("My words"), they are sent with the recording to improve recognition.

3. Rewrite, correction and translation

These features only run when you ask for them — by tapping the corresponding keyboard button, or by dropping the floating bubble on Translate, Correct or Emojis (section 7):

  • Rewrite, correction and emojis: the text of the field you are typing in is sent to Groq (United States) to be rewritten, corrected or enriched with emojis, then returned to you.
  • Translation: the text is sent to Microsoft Translator (Microsoft Azure).

Our server neither stores nor logs this text, nor the text of your dictations.

4. Device identifier and usage data

On first launch, the app creates a device identifier derived from your phone's Android ID. It is used to apply the free daily limit, to unlock your subscription and to prevent abuse. With it, our server stores:

  • app version, phone model, Android version and country (deduced from the connection, not GPS), kept until you delete your data;
  • your plan (free or premium) and, if you subscribe, the Google Play purchase reference, so your subscription can be restored (kept up to 2 years);
  • usage counters per day — number of dictations and words, audio duration, languages and modes used, response time (kept 90 days).

When a new device registers, its IP address is kept for at most 25 hours to limit abuse. We do not sell or share this data.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google, to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. They receive events such as the first dictation, the use of rewrite or translation, setup steps, purchases (plan, price), the store campaign that brought you to the app, crash reports and technical information about your device. Firebase may use your phone's advertising identifier; you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads). DictoKey contains no advertising and does not use this data for advertising.

Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

6. Data stored on your phone

The app keeps on your phone your settings, your personal words and a history of your last 50 dictations and translations. You can clear the history in the app. Like other app settings, they can be included in your Android backup if backup is enabled on your phone.

7. Floating Bubble & Accessibility Service (Optional)

DictoKey offers an optional floating bubble feature that lets you dictate or translate text and insert it directly into any app — without switching keyboards. This feature is disabled by default and must be explicitly activated from the app Settings.

When you enable the floating bubble, DictoKey requests access to Android's Accessibility Service. Before granting this permission, the app displays a disclosure screen explaining exactly what the permission allows.

What the Accessibility Service is used for:

  • Inserting your dictated or translated text into the focused text field of the app you're currently using.
  • Reading the state of the focused field (empty, filled, password-type) to choose the right action.
  • Reading the text of the focused field only when you drop the bubble on Translate, Correct or Emojis, to transform it as described in section 3 — the same processing as the keyboard buttons. The bubble asks for your consent before the first use. Its undo history (Previous / Next) stays in memory on your phone and is never sent.
  • Detecting when a sensitive app is in the foreground and automatically blocking insertion to protect you.

What DictoKey never does with this permission:

  • We do not record or log anything you type on your system keyboard, and we transmit nothing you type except the field text you explicitly send to Translate, Correct or Emojis.
  • We do not read or store the content of messages, emails, screens, or any app content outside the bubble's own dictation flow and the field text you choose to transform.
  • We do not access content from passwords, banking apps, or any app in our sensitive-apps blocklist.
  • We do not send any app content to external services, except the field text you choose to transform (section 3), which we do not keep.

Sensitive apps blocklist — DictoKey maintains an internal blocklist of 95 sensitive apps (banks, password managers, crypto wallets, and payment apps across French, Indonesian, US, and UK markets). When any of these apps is in the foreground, the bubble refuses to insert text and displays a warning. This blocklist is built into the app and cannot be bypassed. It applies to the bubble; the DictoKey keyboard can still be used in these apps, like any keyboard.

How to disable: You can disable the floating bubble at any time via the toggle in the app's Settings > "Floating bubble" section. You can additionally revoke the Accessibility permission from Android's own Settings > Accessibility > DictoKey.

The DictoKey keyboard reads the text of the field you are typing in only when you tap Rewrite, Correct, Undo or Clear; only Rewrite and Correct send it to our server (section 3).

8. Security

All communications between the app, our server and the services above are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our server runs on Cloudflare.

9. Your rights and deletion (GDPR)

  • "Delete my data" in the app settings deletes your device record on our server (identifier, plan, access token, daily limits) and the identifier stored on your phone.
  • Usage counters (section 4) are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have them deleted sooner, or to delete your Firebase analytics data, write to us with the code shown in the app (Home > "Your code").
  • You can also ask us what data is linked to your device, or object to its use, at the address below.

10. Children

DictoKey is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. DictoKey for Windows (desktop app)

The Windows app works like the Android app, with these differences:

  • Dictation, rewrite and translation go through a separate DictoKey server and use the same services described in sections 2 and 3 (Microsoft Azure, Groq, Microsoft Translator). Audio is never written to storage and our server does not store or log the text you dictate. Words from your personal dictionary are sent with the recording to improve recognition.
  • Device identifier: a random identifier is created on first launch (it is not derived from your hardware). Our server keeps it with an access token, your plan, and usage counters (number of words and seconds of audio per period, kept at most 13 months) to apply the free limit. To limit abuse, the number of new devices per network is counted for at most 25 hours.
  • Subscription and licence: payments are handled by Polar, which acts as the seller (merchant of record); we never see your payment details. To deliver and check your licence, our server stores your email address, your licence key and the status of your subscription. The licence key is sent to you by email through Resend.
  • On your computer: your settings, personal dictionary and dictation history are stored locally and are never uploaded. You can clear the history in the app; uninstalling the app does not delete them automatically.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting: the Windows app does not use Google Firebase or any other analytics service. It keeps a technical log on your computer that does not contain your dictated text, and it checks for updates on GitHub.
  • To delete your licence or server data, write to us at the address below.
