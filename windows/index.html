DictoKey for Windows

Voice dictation for your PC · Updated: October 20, 2026

Press a shortcut, speak, and your words are typed where your cursor is — in your email, a document, a chat or a browser. Punctuation and capitals are added for you, and you can translate or rewrite what you said. Same engine as DictoKey for Android.

Download for Windows (free) Also on Android

On the download page, choose the file ending in x64-setup.exe. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

Also on your Android phone. Scan this code with your phone to get DictoKey on Google Play: a voice keyboard and a floating bubble that work in most apps.

How it works

  1. Put your cursor in any text field.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space and speak. Press it again to finish, or simply stop talking: recording stops after a few seconds of silence.
  3. Your text appears in the field. If no field is selected, it is copied to the clipboard.

To dictate and translate in one go, use Ctrl + Alt + T. Both shortcuts can be changed in the settings.

What you get

  • Clean text: punctuation and capitals restored automatically, so a dictated message needs no manual editing.
  • 51 languages, with automatic language detection and real-time translation.
  • AI rewrite of what you dictated.
  • Personal dictionary for names and words you use often, and a local history of your dictations.
  • Runs quietly in the system tray, starts with Windows if you want, and updates itself.
  • Interface in English and French.

Windows may show a warning the first time

The installer is not yet signed with a Microsoft certificate, so Windows SmartScreen may display « Windows protected your PC ». Click More info, then Run anyway. Updates are signed and checked by the app before they are installed.

Privacy

DictoKey needs an internet connection: speech recognition, rewrite and translation run in the cloud. Recordings are never stored, and your history and dictionary stay on your computer. Details: privacy policy, section 11.
