2 ways to dictate, plus shortcuts

Your choice, every time.

The same voice engine, two independent ways to use it: if one is not available, the other still works. Notification shortcuts are a bonus.

Floating bubbleNew

Appears whenever you open a keyboard. Tap to dictate, long press to translate. Drag it onto Translate, Correct or Emojis to rework what you already wrote, and step back just like on the keyboard. If a dictation can’t be inserted, the bubble keeps it for a few minutes and offers to insert it in the next field you tap.

Voice IME keyboard

A dedicated voice keyboard with big Dictate and Translate buttons, punctuation and undo, available in every app where you type.

Notification shortcuts (optional)

If you allow notifications: switch languages, show or hide the bubble, open the interpreter, translate the clipboard or copy your last dictation. Not needed to dictate.
