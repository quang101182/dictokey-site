1. The service

DictoKey turns your voice into text, and can rewrite and translate it. It needs an internet connection: speech recognition, rewriting and translation run in the cloud.

2. Plans and limits

PlanLimits
Android, Free30 dictations per day and 3 hours of dictation per month.
Android, Premium1,000 dictations per day and 20 hours of dictation per month.
Windows, Free2,000 words per week and 1 hour of dictation per month.
Windows, PremiumNo weekly word limit, 20 hours of dictation per month.

Hours count the length of the audio sent for transcription. Android limits apply per device, Windows limits per licence. Daily limits restart at midnight UTC, monthly limits on the 1st of each month at midnight UTC.

On Android, the app warns you at 80% and 95% of your monthly limit. Once it is reached, dictation stops until the 1st of the next month. Rewriting and translating text you already have keep working.
